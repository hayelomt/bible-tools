# Lamp 2.1

*29 October 2026*

## Enduring Word commentary

A verse chip can now open David Guzik's Enduring Word commentary on its chapter, beside Blue Letter Bible, YouVersion, Bible Hub and Logos. New accounts list it straight away. If you already have an account, turn it on in **Settings → Open in other apps**, and drag it where you want it.

## Density and text size

**Settings → Appearance** has two new choices, kept for each device so your phone and laptop can differ:

- **Density:** Dense, Compact, Default or Roomy, for how much fits on the screen.
- **Text size:** Tiny, Small, Default, Large or Larger, for notes, scripture and everything around them.

Buttons stay large enough to tap at every setting.

## Updating

The app updates itself the next time you open it while online. This release changes what syncs, so an older copy says **Update required**: reload the page. Your notes stay on your device and sync once it has updated.
