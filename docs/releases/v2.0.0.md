# Lamp 2.0

*19 October 2026*

Lamp is a place to keep what you learn from sermons, Bible studies and your own reading. It works offline on your phone and laptop, and finds any note by the verse it touches. Version 2.0 is the first release. It's everything from the first version, plus four new things: a way in for verses from other apps, a way to think across your notes, a sermon workflow, and studying with others.

## Study with a group

- **Groups.** Start a group around one or more of your series and send the invite link to the people you study with. Each lesson then has a **group thread**, which every member sees, beside **My notes**, which stay yours alone. A section can move between the two.
- **Comments and questions.** Comment on each other's sections, and ask the group questions. **Questions** on the group's page lists every open question across its series, with who asked and who answered.
- **What's new.** The group's page lists replies to your sections, comments and new questions, and the group's row says how many are new. You can also turn on a weekly email of the same in Settings → Groups. Every email has a link to stop them.
- **Leaving.** If you leave a group, or the owner removes you, what you added goes back to your own notes.

## Share a link

Share a lesson, a series, everything you've written on a passage, or a lesson's one-page handout, as a read-only link anyone can open, with or without an account. A link can expire after a set time, and you can revoke it whenever you like. **Settings → Shared links** lists them all and shows when each was last opened.

## Also new since the first version

- **Verses from other apps.** Share a verse from YouVersion or another Bible app, and it lands in your inbox as a verse chip, two taps from a lesson. Pasted references and verse text become chips too. A chip can open the passage in another Bible app.
- **Thinking across notes.** Open a passage to see every note you've written on it, across every lesson. Link notes with `[[`. Follow a question across the lessons that asked it again or answered it. Start a lesson from a study-method template: SOAP, inductive, Lectio Divina, verse mapping, or your own.
- **Sermons.** Capture mode for taking notes while someone preaches. Timestamps that open the recording at that moment. Inbox triage during the week. A prep card for next Sunday's passage.
- **Looking back.** Review brings back last year's lessons, verses you keep returning to, and questions still open. A theme map shows your tags by month and by book, and a digest sums up each quarter. Export a one-page handout, or Markdown ready to paste into Notion.

## Privacy

Your notes are yours. Only what you share is visible to others: a share link shows exactly what it names, and a group sees only its group thread, never your own notes. The **privacy note** (Settings → About) says what is stored, who can see it, and how to delete it. The **user guide** sits beside it there.

## Updating

The app updates itself the next time you open it while online. If it says **Update required**, reload the page. Your notes stay on your device and sync once it has updated.
