# Code of conduct

Lamp follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and anywhere someone
represents the project.

In short: be kind and patient, assume good intent, welcome people of every background and belief,
and keep disagreements about the work. Harassment, insults, and publishing someone's private
information are not accepted.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour worries you, contact a maintainer privately rather than in a public issue.
The maintainers are listed on the repository's GitHub page, and each one's profile says how to
reach them. Reports are kept confidential, and the maintainers follow the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines)
in deciding what to do, from a private word to a ban from the project.
